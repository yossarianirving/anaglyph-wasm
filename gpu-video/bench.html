WebGPU pipeline benchmark

Times the all-GPU pipeline end to end (demux → decode → pair → GPU blend → capture → encode → mux) plus decode-only and encode-only bounds. Mirrors the window.__benchResults contract of the WebCodecs bench page, so bench/run-web-bench.mjs --page drives both. First iteration includes warm-up costs; the harness reports medians. Use “Copy JSON” to grab results on a phone.
